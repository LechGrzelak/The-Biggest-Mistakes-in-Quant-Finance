# The Biggest Mistakes in Quant Finance

A collection of lecture slides by **Lech A. Grzelak** that explores the most common conceptual, mathematical, numerical, and modeling mistakes made in quantitative finance. Rather than focusing on formulas alone, this series examines how seemingly reasonable assumptions can lead to incorrect pricing, misleading risk measures, flawed hedging strategies, unstable calibrations, and costly financial decisions.

## Overview

Quantitative finance is built on sophisticated mathematics, probability theory, numerical methods, and financial modeling. Yet many real-world failures originate not from advanced mathematics, but from violations of fundamental principles.

This slide deck presents **30 of the most important mistakes in quantitative finance**, covering topics such as:

- Risk-neutral valuation and measure changes
- Stochastic calculus and model assumptions
- Volatility misconceptions and smile dynamics
- Calibration and model validation
- Monte Carlo and Fourier pricing methods
- Hedging and risk management pitfalls
- Interest-rate and yield-curve modeling
- Forward rates, convexity adjustments, and multi-curve frameworks
- Counterparty risk and exposure measurement
- Value-at-Risk and tail-risk interpretation

Each topic is structured around:

**Incorrect Belief → Quant Mistake → Mathematical Principle → Examples → Quant Rule**

The objective is not simply to identify mistakes, but to develop a repeatable framework for recognizing, diagnosing, correcting, and preventing them in practice. 

## What You'll Learn

By studying this material, you will learn how to:

- Detect look-ahead bias and data leakage
- Distinguish pricing measures from forecasting measures
- Understand when model assumptions break down
- Avoid common simulation and discretization errors
- Interpret implied volatility correctly
- Validate calibrations beyond goodness-of-fit metrics
- Build more robust hedging and risk-management frameworks
- Recognize when numerical convergence does not imply correctness
- Understand the limitations of popular financial models

## Key Message

> The most valuable skill in quantitative finance is not knowing a formula, but knowing when a formula is being used incorrectly.

Many quant disasters begin with a simple conceptual error hidden inside a sophisticated model. This lecture series helps practitioners develop the critical thinking required to identify those errors before they become expensive mistakes.
